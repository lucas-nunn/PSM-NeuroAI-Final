Why both normalized metrics?

signedv = rv / √NCv
squaredv = rv² / NCv

Signed scores expose anti-prediction. Squared scores match the Algonauts 2023 predictable-variance convention. Normalize each target first, then average targets and folds.

No clipping

A normalized estimate above 1 can occur because both prediction accuracy and ceilings are finite-sample estimates. Preserve it for diagnosis—do not silently cap it.
